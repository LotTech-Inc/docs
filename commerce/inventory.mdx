The Inventory module is your central hub for managing everything your dealership sells — from retail accessories to service parts. You can add items one at a time, import a full catalog via CSV, edit prices and quantities directly in the table, and apply bulk price adjustments across your entire inventory in seconds.

<Note>
  Your role and the products enabled at your dealership determine which inventory pages you can open. Ask your rooftop admin if the inventory section you need is missing.
</Note>

## Retail vs. Parts Inventory

LotTech Voice maintains two separate inventory views to keep retail items and service parts organized:

- **Inventory** — retail items such as accessories, merchandise, and add-on products. Accessible from **Inventory** in the sidebar.
- **Parts Inventory** — OEM and aftermarket service parts. Accessible from **Parts Inventory** in the sidebar.

Both views use the same interface and feature set. The sections below apply equally to both.

## Adding Items

### Add an Individual Item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Item modal">
    Click the **Add Item** button in the top-right corner of the inventory table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the item details">
    Enter the item's **name**, **SKU**, **price**, and **quantity**. All fields are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the item">
    Click **Save**. The item appears in the table immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Bulk Import via CSV

If you're adding many items at once, upload a CSV file instead of entering items individually.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the template (optional)">
    Click **Upload CSV**, then click **Download Template** to get a pre-formatted file with the correct column headers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your file">
    Fill in your item data — name, SKU, price, quantity, and type — following the template format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Click **Upload CSV**, select your completed file, and confirm. LotTech Voice imports all rows and flags any errors for review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing Items

Every cell in the inventory table is editable inline — no separate edit screen required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a cell">
    Click any editable cell (such as price or quantity) in the table row you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new value">
    Type the updated value directly in the cell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    Press **Enter** or click outside the cell to save. The table updates instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Setting an item's quantity to **0** will prompt a confirmation dialog before saving. Confirming removes the item from the active inventory list. You can re-add it at any time.
</Note>

## Bulk Price Updates

To apply a percentage price change across all items (or a filtered subset) at once:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Bulk Price Update">
    Click the **Bulk Price Update** button above the inventory table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the adjustment percentage">
    Type a percentage value. Use a **positive number** to increase prices (e.g., `5` for +5%) or a **negative number** to decrease them (e.g., `-10` for −10%).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the update">
    Review the summary and click **Apply**. All matching items are updated immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Bulk Price Update applies to all items currently shown in the table. Use the search bar or filters first to narrow the affected set before running the update.
</Info>

## Deleting Items

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the item">
    Find the item you want to remove using the search bar or by scrolling the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the delete icon">
    Click the **trash icon** on the right side of the item's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Click **Delete** in the confirmation dialog. The item is permanently removed from inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Searching and Pagination

Use the **search bar** at the top of the table to find items by name or SKU. Results update as you type.

The inventory table paginates at **50 items per page**. Use the pagination controls at the bottom of the table to move between pages. Your current search and filter state is preserved as you paginate.
