The Rental Inventory module keeps your loaner and rental fleet organized and visible — both to your team and to the AI voice agent that answers calls on your behalf. When a customer calls asking about a rental or loaner vehicle, your voice agent draws on this data to give accurate, up-to-date answers.

<Note>
  The Rental module must be enabled on your rooftop by your LotTech administrator. If you don't see **Rental Inventory** in your sidebar, contact LotTech support to have the module activated.
</Note>

## Accessing Rental Inventory

Navigate to **Rental Inventory** in the left sidebar to open the rental fleet management page. This option only appears when the `rental` module is enabled on your rooftop.

## Fleet Overview

Your home dashboard includes a **Rental** stat card that displays your total fleet size at a glance. This gives managers and service advisors an immediate count of vehicles available without navigating to the full inventory page.

Check this card regularly to ensure the fleet count stays accurate — especially after adding or removing vehicles.

## Managing Vehicles

From the Rental Inventory page, you can add, update, and remove vehicles in your fleet:

- **Add a vehicle** — click **Add Vehicle** and fill in the vehicle details (year, make, model, and any additional notes)
- **Update a vehicle** — click a vehicle record to edit its details, availability status, or notes
- **Remove a vehicle** — use the delete option on a vehicle record to retire it from the fleet

Keep your rental inventory current so the data your voice agent references is always accurate.

<Tip>
  Fleet size and availability data from Rental Inventory feeds directly into the AI voice agent. An up-to-date fleet list means your agent can accurately tell callers how many rentals are available — reducing hold times and improving the caller experience.
</Tip>
