The Rental Inventory module keeps your loaner and rental fleet organized and visible — both to your team and to the AI voice agent that answers calls on your behalf. When a customer calls asking about a rental or loaner vehicle, your voice agent draws on this data to give accurate, up-to-date answers.

<Note>
  The Rental module must be enabled on your rooftop by your LotTech administrator. If you don't see **Rental Inventory** in your sidebar, contact LotTech support to have the module activated.
</Note>

## Accessing Rental Inventory

Navigate to **Rental Inventory** in the left sidebar to open the rental fleet management page. The table displays each vehicle's VIN, year, make, model, and trim. The total fleet count appears in the page header.

## Adding Vehicles

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Vehicles modal">
    Click the **Add Vehicles** button in the top-right corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter vehicle VINs">
    Add vehicles by VIN — either one at a time or in bulk. LotTech Voice automatically looks up year, make, model, and trim from the VIN you provide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Save** or **Add** to add the vehicles to your fleet. They appear in the table immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Fleet vehicle data feeds directly into the AI voice agent. An up-to-date fleet list means your agent can accurately tell callers how many rentals are available — reducing hold times and improving the caller experience.
</Tip>

## Viewing and Editing Vehicle Details

Click any vehicle row to open the **Vehicle Details** panel. From here you can:

- View the full VIN, year, make, model, and trim
- Add or edit a **description** or notes for the vehicle
- **Re-Enrich** the vehicle to refresh its data from the VIN lookup service if details are missing or outdated

<Note>
  The VIN is the primary identifier for each vehicle. Make, model, and trim are populated automatically — you don't need to enter them manually.
</Note>

## Re-Enriching Vehicle Data

If a vehicle's year, make, model, or trim is missing or incorrect, you can trigger a fresh lookup:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the vehicle">
    Find the vehicle in the table using the search bar or by scrolling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-Enrich">
    Click the **Re-Enrich** action on the vehicle row (or open the vehicle details and click **Re-Enrich** there). LotTech Voice fetches updated data for that VIN and saves it automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing Vehicles

**To remove a single vehicle:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the vehicle">
    Find the vehicle in the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete it">
    Click the **delete icon** on the vehicle row and confirm. The vehicle is removed from your fleet immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**To remove multiple vehicles at once:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select vehicles">
    Check the checkbox next to each vehicle you want to remove. Use the header checkbox to select all vehicles on the current page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete selected">
    Click **Delete Selected** in the selection toolbar that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the deletion in the dialog. All selected vehicles are removed at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting vehicles from Rental Inventory is permanent. Removed vehicles will no longer be available to the AI voice agent when answering caller questions about fleet availability.
</Warning>

## Searching and Pagination

Use the **search bar** at the top of the table to find vehicles by VIN, make, model, or other details. Results update as you type.

The rental inventory table paginates at **50 vehicles per page**. Use the pagination controls at the bottom to move between pages.
