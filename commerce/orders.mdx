The Orders module gives you a complete picture of every customer purchase at your dealership — from the moment an order is placed through fulfillment and delivery. Use it to monitor revenue, track fulfillment progress, and keep your team aligned on what needs attention.

<Note>
  Orders is available for dealerships using Commerce and team members with access to that area. Ask your rooftop admin if Orders is missing from your sidebar.
</Note>

## Order Statuses

Every order moves through a defined lifecycle. The table below describes what each status means so your team always knows where an order stands.

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | The order has been placed and is awaiting processing. |
| **Processing** | The order is being prepared (picked, packed, or scheduled). |
| **Shipped** | The order has left your facility and is in transit to the customer. |
| **Complete** | The order has been delivered and fulfilled. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled and will not be fulfilled. |

## Browsing Orders

Navigate to **Orders** in the left sidebar to open the orders list. At the top of the page, a stats strip gives you an at-a-glance summary of your activity:

- **Total** — the total number of orders across all statuses
- **Needs Action** — orders in a Confirmed or Processing state that require attention
- **Shipped** — orders actively in transit to customers
- **Complete** — orders that have been fully fulfilled
- **Revenue** — combined revenue across all non-cancelled orders

Each row in the list shows the order number, customer name, fulfillment type, item count, total, and status.

## Filtering and Searching

Use the filter controls above the orders list to narrow your view:

- **Status filter** — select one or more statuses (Confirmed, Processing, Shipped, Complete, Cancelled) to show only matching orders
- **Date range** — pick a preset range or a custom start and end date to limit results to a specific time window
- **Search bar** — type an order number or customer name to find a specific order instantly

Combine filters and search to zero in on exactly what you need — for example, all **Shipped** orders from last week for a particular customer.

## Order Details

Click any order in the list to open its detail page. The detail view includes:

- **Customer information** — name, contact details, and associated account
- **Line items** — each product or service ordered, with unit price and quantity
- **Order total** — the final charged amount displayed in currency format
- **Fulfillment status** — current status and any status history
- **Timestamps** — when the order was placed, last updated, and completed

Use the detail page to verify what was ordered and confirm fulfillment milestones with your team.

## Archiving Orders

Once an order is complete or cancelled, you can archive it to keep your active orders list focused on work in progress. Archived orders can be restored at any time from the Archived Orders view.

**To archive a single order:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the order">
    Click the order in the list to open its detail page, or click the **Archive** action on the order row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive it">
    Click the **Archive** button and confirm. The order moves out of the active list immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**To bulk archive multiple orders:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select orders">
    Check the checkbox next to each order you want to archive. You can select all visible orders using the header checkbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger bulk archive">
    Click the **Archive** button that appears in the toolbar when one or more orders are checked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the action in the dialog. All selected orders are archived at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To review archived orders, click **Archived Orders** in the top-right of the Orders page. Archived orders can be restored from that view and are always retained for your records.
