Connecting Stripe to LotTech Voice enables your customer-facing parts storefront to accept payments online and routes revenue data into the Orders module for tracking. The setup process guides you through Stripe's own onboarding flow and takes only a few minutes to complete.

## Prerequisites

Before connecting Stripe, confirm the following:

- You have a **Stripe account**, or you're prepared to create one during the onboarding flow. Stripe accounts are free to create at [stripe.com](https://stripe.com).
- The **Commerce module** is enabled on your rooftop. If you don't see the Commerce tab in Rooftop Settings, contact your LotTech administrator.
- You have **manager-level access** in LotTech Voice. The Stripe setup section is only available to users with the manager role.

## Connecting Stripe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rooftop Settings">
    Click your rooftop name or the settings icon to open **Rooftop Settings**, then navigate to the **Commerce** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Click **Set Up Stripe** in the Stripe Payments section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Stripe's onboarding">
    You'll be redirected to Stripe to set up or link your account. Follow all prompts — including business details, bank account information, and identity verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to LotTech Voice">
    After completing Stripe's onboarding, you'll be redirected back to LotTech Voice. Your Stripe status updates automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you leave Stripe's onboarding before completing all steps, your status will show as **Setup Incomplete**. Return to **Rooftop Settings → Commerce** and click **Resume Setup** to pick up where you left off.
</Note>

## Stripe Account Status

Check the Stripe Payments section of the Commerce tab at any time to see your current connection status.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not Configured** | No Stripe account has been linked to this rooftop. |
| **Setup Incomplete** | Stripe onboarding has been started but not fully completed. Charges are not yet enabled. |
| **Payments Enabled** | Onboarding is complete and charges are enabled. Your storefront can accept payments. |

Payments will only process when the status is **Payments Enabled**.

## Tax Setup

After your Stripe account is connected and onboarding is complete, you need to configure tax settings in Stripe and then confirm the setup in LotTech Voice before payments are fully activated.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure taxes in Stripe">
    In your [Stripe Tax Settings](https://dashboard.stripe.com/settings/tax), set up the appropriate tax rates and collection rules for your location and products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in LotTech Voice">
    Back in **Rooftop Settings → Commerce**, click **Confirm Tax Setup** once your Stripe tax settings are correct. LotTech Voice will verify the configuration with Stripe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  After confirming tax setup, Stripe may still need to review your account before payments are fully enabled. If you see a "Waiting for Stripe Approval" message, no further action is required — Stripe will complete the review automatically.
</Note>

## GA4 Analytics

A LotTech administrator can optionally connect Google Analytics 4 to track storefront activity such as page views, product views, and purchases. This setting is managed at the admin level in Rooftop Settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your Measurement ID">
    In your Google Analytics 4 property, go to **Admin → Data Streams** and copy the **Measurement ID** (formatted as `G-XXXXXXXXXX`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Measurement ID">
    In **Rooftop Settings → Commerce**, paste your Measurement ID into the **GA4 Measurement ID** field and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once connected, storefront events will appear in your GA4 property within 24–48 hours.

## Embed Configuration

Once your Stripe account is active, you can embed the parts storefront directly on your dealership's website. Configure the allowed domains to ensure the embed loads correctly and securely.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Embed Config">
    In **Rooftop Settings → Commerce**, locate the **Embed Configuration** section. This section only appears after Stripe is fully active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the embed">
    Toggle **Embed Enabled** to on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain(s)">
    Enter each domain that will host the embedded storefront (for example, `www.yourdealer.com`). Add one domain at a time and click **Add** after each entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the embed code">
    Once at least one domain is added and the embed is enabled, click **Copy** to copy the embed snippet. Paste it into the HTML of your dealership website where you want the storefront to appear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnecting Stripe

If you need to unlink your Stripe account, contact your LotTech administrator. Administrators can remove the Stripe account from **Rooftop Settings → Commerce**.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting Stripe immediately disables payment processing on your storefront. Customers will not be able to complete purchases until a Stripe account is reconnected. Ensure all outstanding orders are fulfilled or otherwise resolved before disconnecting.
</Warning>
