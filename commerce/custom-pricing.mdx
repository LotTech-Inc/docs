Custom Pricing lets you offer different prices to specific customers or groups — such as fleet account holders, wholesale buyers, or preferred service clients — without altering the standard prices in your inventory. You do this through two building blocks: **Customer Groups** and **Discount Rules**.

<Note>
  Custom Pricing is available to team members with Commerce access at a dealership where Commerce is enabled. Ask your rooftop admin if this section is missing.
</Note>

## How Custom Pricing Works

Custom pricing is built from two components:

- **Customer Groups** — named segments of customers (for example, "Wholesale", "Fleet Accounts"). A customer can belong to only one group per rooftop.
- **Discount Rules** — percentage or fixed-amount discounts that apply to a group or an individual customer. A rule can target all inventory items or a single specific item.

When a customer in a group places an order on the storefront, any active discount rules for their group are applied automatically at checkout. Customers not in any group continue to pay standard prices.

## Customer Groups

### Creating a Group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Group form">
    Click **New Group** in the Customer Groups section of the Custom Pricing page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the group">
    Enter a **Group Name** (for example, "Wholesale" or "Fleet Accounts") and an optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. The group appears in the list immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Adding Customers to a Group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the group">
    Click a group in the list to expand its member management panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a customer email">
    Type the customer's email address in the field and click **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A customer can only belong to one group per rooftop. Adding a customer to a new group automatically moves them out of any previous group.
</Note>

### Removing a Customer from a Group

Click the **remove icon** next to any member in the expanded group panel and confirm. The customer reverts to standard (base) pricing immediately.

### Deleting a Group

Click the **delete icon** on a group row and confirm. Deleting a group removes all its members and any discount rules associated with it.

## Discount Rules

### Creating a Discount Rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Rule form">
    Click **Add Rule** in the Discount Rules section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the rule">
    Enter a descriptive **Rule Name** (for example, "Wholesale 15% Off"). This name is visible to customers on their orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a target">
    Choose whether the rule applies to a **Customer Group** or a **Specific Customer** (by email address).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scope to an item (optional)">
    Optionally search for and select a specific inventory item. If you leave this blank, the rule applies to all items in the storefront.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the discount">
    Choose a discount type:
    - **Percentage (%)** — deduct a percentage of the item price (for example, 15 for 15% off)
    - **Fixed Amount ($)** — deduct a flat dollar amount from the item price

    Enter the discount value. A preview of the discounted price appears automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. The rule is active immediately and applies the next time a matched customer views or purchases items on the storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Managing Rules

All active and inactive discount rules are listed in the Discount Rules section. From here you can:

- **Enable or disable a rule** — click the **Active / Inactive** toggle on a rule row to turn it on or off without deleting it
- **Edit a rule** — click the **edit icon** on a rule row to update its name, item scope, discount type, or value
- **Delete a rule** — click the **delete icon** on a rule row and confirm. The rule is removed immediately and affected customers revert to standard pricing.

<Tip>
  Use the Active/Inactive toggle to temporarily suspend a discount (for example, at the end of a promotional period) without losing the rule configuration.
</Tip>
