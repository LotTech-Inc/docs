Custom Pricing lets you offer different prices to specific customers or organizations — such as fleet account holders, wholesale buyers, or preferred service clients — without altering the standard prices in your inventory. Rules are applied automatically on the customer-facing storefront when a matched customer places an order.

<Note>
  Custom Pricing requires the `commerce` module permission on your rooftop. Contact your LotTech administrator if this option is not visible in your sidebar.
</Note>

## What Is Custom Pricing?

Standard inventory prices apply to all customers by default. Custom Pricing gives you the flexibility to create exceptions — assigning a specific price for a particular item to a specific customer or organization. This is useful for:

- **Fleet accounts** that negotiate fixed per-unit pricing on frequently ordered parts
- **Wholesale buyers** who receive volume-based or margin-adjusted rates
- **Preferred service customers** with contractual pricing agreements

Custom pricing rules override the standard item price only for the matched customer. All other customers continue to see and pay the default price.

## Accessing Custom Pricing

Navigate to **Custom Pricing** in the left sidebar to open the pricing rules page. The `commerce` module permission is required to view and manage this section.

## Creating a Pricing Rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the new rule form">
    Click **Add Pricing Rule** on the Custom Pricing page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the customer or organization">
    Search for and select the customer account or organization this rule applies to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the item">
    Search your inventory and select the specific part or retail item this price applies to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the custom price">
    Enter the custom price for this customer–item combination. This price will replace the standard inventory price for that customer at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rule">
    Click **Save**. The rule is active immediately and will apply the next time the matched customer views or purchases that item on the storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Rules

All active pricing rules are listed on the Custom Pricing page. From here you can:

- **Edit a rule** — click the rule row to modify the customer, item, or price
- **Delete a rule** — click the delete icon on a rule row and confirm. The item reverts to standard pricing for that customer immediately.

Review your pricing rules periodically to ensure custom rates are still accurate and aligned with your current agreements.

<Note>
  Custom pricing rules only take effect on the customer-facing storefront. When you view order details in the LotTech Voice dashboard, you will always see the actual price the customer was charged — which may differ from the standard inventory price when a custom rule applied.
</Note>
