**For rooftop admins completing a new dealership setup.** When your invitation opens the setup wizard, work through the steps below.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your dealership details">
    Check your dealership name, contact phone, timezone, and address. For a dealership using the receptionist, also check the agent name and department phone numbers shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set business hours">
    Enter your opening and closing times. These hours help the receptionist explain when your dealership is open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add holiday closures">
    Add dates when your dealership will be closed outside its usual schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Enter each teammate's email address and select the role they need. They receive a link to set up their own account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your receptionist">
    If your dealership uses the receptionist, listen to the voice preview. Commerce-only dealerships finish with the **All set** step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can return to these settings from **Rooftop Settings**. Completing this wizard updates your dealership details; contact LotTech support if a purchased communication channel still needs activation.

After your team joins, check [email notifications](/account/email-notifications) and [voice transfers](/rooftop/phone-and-transfers).
