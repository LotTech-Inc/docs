**For rooftop admins.** Set your dealership's opening hours and holiday closures from **Rooftop Settings → Operations**.

Managers can check the published settings and ask their rooftop admin to make changes.

## Update business hours

1. Confirm that you have selected the correct dealership.
2. Open **Rooftop Settings → Operations**.
3. Select **Edit** in **Business Hours**.
4. Set opening and closing times for each day your dealership is open. Leave closed days without times.
5. Select **Save Hours**.

Review the displayed schedule after saving. Check your dealership's timezone as well if opening or closing times appear incorrect.

## Add holiday closures

Closed dates override your normal weekly opening hours.

1. Open **Closed Dates** on the **Operations** tab and select **Edit**.
2. Add the dates or named holidays when your dealership will be closed.
3. Select **Save Changes**.

Review this list before holidays or other planned closures. Remove or update entries when your plans change.

## Service transport options

If your dealership uses manual callbacks or Google Calendar for service scheduling, the **Transport Options** section lets you maintain the options available to customers. Only include services your dealership offers.

When using Xtime or DealerFX, transport options come from the connected scheduling system.
