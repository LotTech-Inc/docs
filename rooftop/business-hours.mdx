Your AI voice agent relies on your rooftop's business hours to give callers accurate information — whether that's confirming today's closing time, letting a customer know the service bay is closed on a holiday, or gracefully handling an after-hours inquiry. Keeping these settings current ensures every caller gets a response that reflects reality.

## Setting Business Hours

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rooftop Settings">
    In the sidebar, navigate to **Rooftop Settings** for your location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Operations tab">
    Click the **Operations** tab at the top of the settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Business Hours section">
    Scroll to **Business Hours** and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set hours for each day">
    For each day of the week, set an **open time** and a **close time** using the time pickers. Leave a day blank if your dealership is closed that day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to apply the updated hours. The AI agent will use the new schedule immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Any day of the week that does not have an open and close time configured is treated as **closed**. If your dealership is open seven days a week, make sure all seven days have times set.
</Info>

## Adding Closed Dates

Closed dates let you block out specific days — like holidays or special events — so the AI agent tells callers the dealership is closed, even if those days would otherwise fall within normal business hours.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Closed Dates">
    In Rooftop Settings > Operations, scroll to the **Closed Dates** section and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a date type">
    Select either a **specific date** (using the date picker) or a **named holiday** from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the holiday or date">
    If you chose a named holiday, select from the available options:

    - Thanksgiving
    - Christmas
    - New Year's Day
    - Independence Day
    - Labor Day
    - Memorial Day
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the closed date">
    Click **Save** to confirm. The date now appears in your closed dates list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Add your closed dates well in advance of the actual holiday. Callers who ask about upcoming availability before the holiday arrives will hear accurate information, not a guess based on your standard schedule.
</Tip>

## Vehicle Makes

The vehicle makes setting tells your AI agent which brands your rooftop sells and services. When a caller asks a brand-specific question — "Do you work on Hondas?" — the agent uses this list to answer confidently and correctly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Vehicle Makes settings">
    In Rooftop Settings > Operations, scroll to **Vehicle Makes** and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or remove makes">
    Type a make name (for example, Toyota, Honda, or Ford) to add it to the list. Click the **×** next to any make to remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** when your list is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If no vehicle makes are configured, the agent treats all makes as allowed. Once you add at least one make, the agent uses your list to answer brand-specific questions accurately.
</Note>

Keep this list accurate and up to date — if your rooftop picks up a new brand franchise or drops one, update Vehicle Makes so the agent reflects the change immediately.

## Transport Options

Transport options define the ways customers can bring their vehicle in — or receive it back — for service department appointments. Common options include shuttle service, loaner vehicles, and standard drop-off.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Transport Options">
    In Rooftop Settings > Operations, scroll to **Transport Options** and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your options">
    Enable or disable each transport type to match the services your service department actually offers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** to apply your selections.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your AI agent uses the configured transport options when discussing service scheduling with callers, so only enable options your team can reliably fulfill.

<Note>
  Transport options are available when your scheduling integration is set to **None (Manual Callback)** or **Google Calendar**. When using a DMS-connected scheduler such as xTime or DealerFX, transport options are sourced directly from that system.
</Note>
