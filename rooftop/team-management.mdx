Every rooftop in LotTech Voice has a team of assigned members who can access its dashboard, handle customer communications, and manage settings. Because access is scoped per rooftop, adding someone to your team gives them visibility into that location only — not the rest of your organization. Only **Admins** and users with team management permissions can modify rooftop team assignments.

## Viewing the Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rooftop Settings">
    In the sidebar, navigate to **Rooftop Settings** for your location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Team tab">
    Select the **Team** tab at the top of the settings page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Team tab lists every member currently assigned to the rooftop, showing each person's **name**, **email address**, and **roles**. It also shows per-member toggles for email notifications and voice call transfers. Use this view to confirm who has access before assigning new members or removing existing ones.

## Assigning a Role to an Existing User

Use this flow when the person already has a LotTech Voice account and you want to give them a role at this rooftop.

<Note>
  Only **Admins** and users with team management permissions can assign roles to a rooftop.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Navigate to Rooftop Settings > **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Role">
    Click the **Add Role** button in the upper area of the Team tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the user">
    Search for and select the person you want to add. Admins can search across all users in the organization; other team managers see only members already at this rooftop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Select the appropriate role from the dropdown. Available roles depend on which modules are active for this rooftop:

    - **Rooftop Admin** — full access to all settings for this rooftop
    - **Parts Manager** — access to parts module (when Parts is active)
    - **Cosmetics Manager** — access to cosmetics module (when Cosmetics is active)
    - **Service Manager** — access to service module (when Service is active)
    - **Commerce Manager** — access to commerce module (when Commerce is active)
    - **Rental Manager** — access to rental module (when Rental is active)
    - **Email Manager** — access to email module (when Email is active)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the assignment">
    Click **Assign Role**. The user now appears in the Team list with their new role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A person can hold multiple roles at the same rooftop. Use the **Add Role** flow again to assign an additional role to someone already on the team.
</Note>

## Creating a New User and Assigning a Role

If the person doesn't yet have a LotTech Voice account, an **Admin** can create their account and assign a role in a single step.

<Note>
  Only **Admins** can create new user accounts.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Navigate to Rooftop Settings > **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add User">
    Click the **Add User** button. This option is visible to Admins only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the user's details">
    Enter the person's first name, last name, email address, password, and optionally a phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Select the role to assign to this person at the current rooftop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the user">
    Click **Create User**. LotTech Voice creates the account and assigns the role to this rooftop in one action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing a Role

<Note>
  Only **Admins** and users with team management permissions can remove roles from a rooftop.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Navigate to Rooftop Settings > **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the member">
    Locate the person whose role you want to remove in the team list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the remove icon on their role">
    Each role appears as a badge next to the person's name. Click the **×** on the role badge you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the removal when prompted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If the role you are removing is the person's **only role** at this rooftop, removing it also removes them from the rooftop entirely, immediately revoking their access to all data for that location — including communications, customers, requests, and logs. You can reassign them at any time using the steps above.
</Warning>

## Member Preferences

Each team member has two per-rooftop preferences you can manage from the Team tab:

- **Email notifications** — when enabled, the member receives email alerts for activity at this rooftop (off by default).
- **Voice transfers** — when enabled, the AI voice agent can transfer live calls to this member (on by default).

To change a preference, use the toggle next to the member's name in the Team list. Changes take effect immediately.

## What Team Members Can Do

Once assigned to a rooftop, a member's available actions depend on their **role and module permissions**. In general, rooftop team members can:

- View the rooftop dashboard and activity feed
- Handle inbound requests and customer communications
- View customer records and conversation histories
- Access call logs and AI interaction transcripts
- Edit rooftop settings within the modules their role covers

For a full breakdown of what each role can access across the platform, see the [Roles and Permissions](/account/roles-and-permissions) guide.
