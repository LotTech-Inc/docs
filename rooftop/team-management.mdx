Every rooftop in LotTech Voice has a team of assigned managers who can access its dashboard, handle customer communications, and manage settings. Because access is scoped per rooftop, adding someone to your team gives them visibility into that location only — not the rest of your organization. Only **Admins** can modify rooftop team assignments.

## Viewing the Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rooftop Settings">
    In the sidebar, navigate to **Rooftop Settings** for your location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Team tab">
    Select the **Team** tab at the top of the settings page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Team tab lists every manager currently assigned to the rooftop, showing each person's **name** and **role**. Use this view to confirm who has access before assigning new members or removing existing ones.

## Assigning a Manager

<Note>
  Only **Admins** can assign managers to a rooftop.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Navigate to Rooftop Settings > **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Assign Manager">
    Click the **Assign Manager** button in the upper area of the Team tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the user">
    Type the person's name into the search field. Results will appear as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the assignment">
    Select the correct user from the results and confirm the assignment. They will now appear in the Team list and have access to this rooftop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The user must already have an account in LotTech Voice before you can assign them to a rooftop. If the person you're looking for doesn't appear in the search results, go to **Admin → Manage Users** to create their account first, then return here to assign them.
</Note>

## Removing a Manager

<Note>
  Only **Admins** can remove managers from a rooftop.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Navigate to Rooftop Settings > **Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the manager">
    Locate the person you want to remove in the team list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the remove action">
    Click the remove icon or action button next to their name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the removal when prompted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Removing a manager from a rooftop immediately revokes their access to **all data for that location** — including communications, customers, requests, and logs. This action takes effect right away. If the removal was a mistake, you can reassign them using the steps above.
</Warning>

## What Team Members Can Do

Once assigned to a rooftop, a manager's available actions depend on their **module permissions**. In general, rooftop team members can:

- View the rooftop dashboard and activity feed
- Handle inbound requests and customer communications
- View customer records and conversation histories
- Access call logs and AI interaction transcripts
- Edit rooftop settings (managers can edit their own assigned rooftops; admins can edit any)

For a full breakdown of what each role can access across the platform, see the [Roles and Permissions](/account/roles-and-permissions) guide.
