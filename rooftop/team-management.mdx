**For rooftop admins.** Use **Rooftop Settings → Team** to manage people at the dealership you have selected.

Managers can view the team information available to them. Team management controls appear for rooftop admins.

## Invite a teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Select your dealership, then open **Rooftop Settings → Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Invite Teammate">
    Enter the teammate's email address and choose their role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Send the invite. A new teammate receives a link to accept the invitation and set their own password and contact details.

    If they already have an account, LotTech grants the selected role and notifies them. They can use their existing sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Available roles include **Rooftop Admin** and manager roles for the products enabled at your dealership. Choose a manager role for day-to-day department work. Choose **Rooftop Admin** when the person also needs to manage the dealership's team and settings.

## Manage pending invitations

New account invitations appear under **Pending Invites** until accepted. Use:

- **Resend** to send a fresh invitation.
- **Revoke** to cancel an invitation that should no longer be used.

If LotTech asks you to wait before resending, wait for the displayed interval before trying again.

## Add a role to a current teammate

1. Select **Add Role**.
2. Choose the teammate from the people already at your rooftop.
3. Select the role you want to add.
4. Select **Assign Role**.

A teammate can have multiple roles at the same dealership. To add someone who is not yet on your team, use **Invite Teammate**.

## Remove a role

Find the teammate, select the **×** beside the role badge, and confirm the removal.

<Warning>
  Removing someone's last role at a rooftop removes their access to that dealership. Check the location and the role before confirming.
</Warning>

## Set teammate preferences

The team list includes two toggles:

- **Email notifications** — alerts for new requests and customer follow-up. See [Email notifications](/account/email-notifications).
- **Voice transfers** — whether the receptionist can transfer callers to the teammate's phone when they ask for that person by name.

Each change saves automatically. These controls update the person's own preferences, which they can also change from **Settings**.

Ask teammates to check their profile phone number before receiving transfers. Department transfer numbers are managed separately under [Phone numbers and transfers](/rooftop/phone-and-transfers).
