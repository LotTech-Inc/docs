A **rooftop** is one dealership location. Customer activity and shared dealership settings belong to the rooftop you are viewing.

## Switch dealerships

If you have access to several locations, select the dealership name at the top of the dashboard and choose a location from the list. Both managers and rooftop admins see only their assigned rooftops.

## Open rooftop settings

Select **Rooftop Settings** in the sidebar. The page has these tabs:

| Tab | What you will find |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Dealership details, contact information, voice settings, department phones, and branding |
| **Operations** | Business hours, closed dates, and available scheduling options |
| **Commerce** | Payment and storefront settings, when Commerce is enabled and available to your role |
| **Team** | Teammates and their roles; team management controls for rooftop admins |

**Rooftop admins** can edit shared dealership settings and manage the team. **Managers** use the available settings views for reference and update their own preferences from **Settings**.
