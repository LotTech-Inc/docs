In LotTech Voice, every dealership location you operate is represented as a **rooftop**. Your rooftop is the central hub for everything your AI voice agent does — from answering inbound calls to scheduling service appointments — and each location you manage gets its own isolated configuration, team, and data.

## What Is a Rooftop?

A rooftop maps one-to-one with a physical dealership location. No two rooftops share settings or data unless you explicitly configure them the same way. Each rooftop owns:

- **Identity** — name, address, email addresses, and phone numbers
- **AI voice agent** — the voice persona, speaking speed, and echo settings callers hear
- **Business hours & closures** — open/close times per day and holiday closed dates
- **Team** — the managers and staff assigned to that location
- **Active modules** — Communications, Commerce, Inventory, and more
- **Phone numbers (DIDs)** — Twilio numbers used for inbound call routing and outbound SMS
- **Brand** — logo and colors displayed on the customer-facing commerce storefront
- **Integrations** — Google Calendar, xTime, DealerFX, and Stripe connections

Because every rooftop is self-contained, changes you make to one location never affect another. You can manage each rooftop at its own pace without worrying about cross-location conflicts.

## Navigating Rooftops

Every page in your LotTech Voice dashboard includes the rooftop **slug** in the URL — for example, `/dashboard/demo-auto/`. The slug is a short, URL-safe identifier for your location.

To switch between rooftops:

1. Look for the **rooftop selector** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the selector to expand a list of all rooftops you are assigned to.
3. Select the rooftop you want to work in — the dashboard reloads scoped to that location.

LotTech Voice remembers the last rooftop you visited, so the next time you log in you'll land right where you left off.

## Rooftop Settings

Access Rooftop Settings from the sidebar under your rooftop's navigation menu. Settings are organized into four tabs:

| Tab | What you configure |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Name, address, contact info, logo, brand colors, AI voice settings, owned phone numbers |
| **Operations** | Business hours, closed dates, supported vehicle makes, transport options, third-party integrations |
| **Commerce** | Stripe payment setup, embeddable widget config, Google Analytics 4 |
| **Team** | Managers assigned to this rooftop |

<Note>
  Only **Managers** and **Admins** can access Rooftop Settings. Admins can view and edit settings for every rooftop in the organization. Managers can only edit the rooftops they are assigned to.
</Note>
