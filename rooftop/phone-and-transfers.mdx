Your receptionist's phone number, department transfer numbers, and personal phone number serve different purposes.

| Number | Purpose | Who manages it |
|---|---|---|
| Receptionist's assigned number | Receives calls handled by LotTech | Contact LotTech support for changes |
| Department phone | Receives transfers to a department, such as Parts or Service | Rooftop admin |
| Profile phone | Receives transfers when a caller asks for you by name | You, from **Settings** |

## Update department numbers

**For rooftop admins.**

1. Open **Rooftop Settings → Overview** for the correct dealership.
2. Find **Department Phones** under **Voice Configuration**.
3. Edit the phone number for the relevant department and save the field.

The departments shown depend on the products enabled at your dealership. Check the number with your team before using it for transfers.

## Set your own transfer preference

Managers and rooftop admins can open **Settings → Notifications** and use **Voice transfers** to allow or stop transfers to their profile phone. Keep that phone number up to date under **Profile**.

## Manage a teammate's preference

Rooftop admins can open **Rooftop Settings → Team** and change the **Voice transfers** toggle beside a teammate. This updates the same personal preference the teammate controls in Settings.

Turning off a person's voice transfers does not change the dealership's department phone numbers.
