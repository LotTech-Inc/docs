Your AI voice agent is the first thing every caller hears when they reach your dealership. A well-tuned voice builds trust immediately — so LotTech Voice gives you full control over the persona, pace, and acoustic character of your agent. This page walks you through every voice setting available on your rooftop.

## Accessing Voice Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rooftop Settings">
    In the left sidebar, navigate to **Rooftop Settings** for your location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Overview tab">
    Click the **Overview** tab at the top of the settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Voice Settings">
    Scroll down to the **Voice Settings** section. All voice, speed, and echo controls are grouped here.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing a Voice

LotTech Voice supports two text-to-speech (TTS) providers: **Rime** and **Cartesia**. Each provider offers a curated set of voices optimized for natural, conversational phone calls.

First select your provider from the **TTS Provider** dropdown, then choose a voice from the **Voice** dropdown that appears.

## Adjusting Voice Speed

The **Voice Speed** slider controls how fast or slow your AI agent speaks. The range runs from **0.6×** (noticeably slower) to **1.5×** (noticeably faster), with **1.0×** as the default baseline.

To change the speed, drag the slider or type a value directly into the speed field, then save your settings.

<Info>
  A speed between **0.8× and 1.1×** tends to sound most natural for dealership calls. Speeds above 1.2× can feel rushed, while speeds below 0.7× may frustrate callers waiting for information.
</Info>

## Echo / Reverb

The **Echo** control adds reverb to your agent's voice — expressed as a percentage from **0% to 100%** in 5% increments. Internally this adjusts both the reverb wet mix and decay time, giving the voice a sense of physical space.

To change the echo level, drag the slider to your desired percentage and save.

<Note>
  Most rooftops leave echo at **0%**. The reverb effect is designed for environments with significant ambient noise or specific acoustic branding preferences. If you're unsure, start at 0% and increase gradually while test-calling your rooftop number.
</Note>

## Phone Number Configuration

The **Owned DIDs** section on the Overview tab lists all Twilio phone numbers currently assigned to your rooftop. These numbers handle inbound call routing and outbound SMS for your AI voice agent.

- **To add or remove a phone number**, contact your LotTech Voice administrator. DID assignment is managed at the organization level and requires admin access.
- **Department forwarding numbers** — for departments such as Parts or Service — can be configured separately so the AI agent can transfer calls to the right team. Ask your administrator to set these up in your rooftop's phone configuration.
