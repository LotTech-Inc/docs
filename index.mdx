## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Your LotTech representative will provision your account and send you login credentials. Check your inbox for the welcome email and follow the link to set your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your rooftop">
    A rooftop represents a single dealership location. Admins can create rooftops from the Admin panel and assign users and modules to each one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your voice agent">
    Navigate to **Rooftop Settings > Overview** to connect your Twilio phone number, select an AI voice, and set your business hours and call-handling preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start handling calls">
    Once your rooftop is configured, the AI voice agent begins answering inbound calls automatically. New interactions, requests, and call logs appear on your dashboard in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
