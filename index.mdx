Use the LotTech dashboard to follow up with customers and manage work at your dealership. These guides are for **managers** and **rooftop admins**.

A **rooftop** is one dealership location. Your dashboard shows the locations assigned to you and the features available for your role.

## Everyday tasks

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Get started" icon="rocket" href="/quickstart">
    Sign in, select your dealership, and find work that needs your attention.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enable email notifications" icon="envelope" href="/account/email-notifications">
    Turn on alerts for new requests and customer follow-up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review customer activity" icon="phone" href="/communications/interactions">
    Read conversations, listen to available recordings, and find related requests.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage requests" icon="list-check" href="/communications/requests">
    Review, assign, and update customer requests.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
