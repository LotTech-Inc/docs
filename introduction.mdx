LotTech Voice is an AI-powered voice agent platform built specifically for automotive dealerships. Instead of missed calls, hold queues, and after-hours voicemails, your dealership gets a live, intelligent agent that answers every inbound call 24/7 — capturing customer needs, creating service and parts requests, and surfacing everything to your team through a single real-time dashboard.

## What LotTech Voice does

When a customer calls your dealership, the AI voice agent picks up immediately, identifies the caller, and guides the conversation to understand what the customer needs — whether that's a service appointment, a parts inquiry, a cosmetic repair estimate, or general information. The agent creates a structured request from the call, logs a full transcript and recording, and routes the request to the right team for follow-up. Your staff sees new interactions appear on the dashboard in real time, so nothing falls through the cracks even outside business hours.

## Multi-rooftop support

A **rooftop** is a single dealership location within your LotTech Voice account. If your organization operates more than one location — such as a service center and a sales lot — each location gets its own rooftop with its own phone number, voice agent configuration, inventory, and team members.

Users can be assigned to one or more rooftops. After you log in, the dashboard opens on your active rooftop. If you have access to multiple locations, use the rooftop switcher in the sidebar to move between them. All data, settings, and permissions are scoped to the rooftop you're currently viewing.

## User roles

LotTech Voice has two user roles. The role assigned to your account controls what you can see and do across the platform.

| Capability | Admin | Manager |
|---|---|---|
| Access all rooftops | ✓ | Assigned rooftops only |
| Create and manage rooftops | ✓ | — |
| Create and manage users | ✓ | — |
| Manage rooftop settings | ✓ | Limited |
| Access Communications module | ✓ | ✓ (if enabled) |
| Access Commerce module | ✓ | ✓ (if enabled) |
| Access Inventory module | ✓ | ✓ (if enabled) |
| Access Analytics module | ✓ | ✓ (if enabled) |

**Admins** have full platform access and are responsible for initial setup, user provisioning, and rooftop configuration. **Managers** work within the rooftops they've been assigned to and can access modules that have been enabled for their role at the rooftop level.

<Note>
  Module availability depends on your dealership's LotTech Voice subscription. Contact your LotTech representative to enable or disable specific modules for any rooftop.
</Note>
