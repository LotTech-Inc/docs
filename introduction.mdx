LotTech brings customer conversations and follow-up work into your dealership dashboard. You can review activity, find requests that need attention, and work with your team to respond.

## Your dealership and your role

A **rooftop** is one dealership location. Each rooftop has its own team, settings, and customer activity.

The two roles covered in these guides are:

- **Manager** — handles day-to-day work for the departments or products assigned to them, such as parts or service.
- **Rooftop admin** — manages the team and dealership settings, as well as the work available at their assigned rooftop.

Both roles are limited to their assigned rooftops. A rooftop admin role at one location does not give you access to every dealership.

If you have access to more than one rooftop, use the dealership selector at the top of the dashboard to switch locations. Confirm the selected dealership before updating settings or working on a request.

## Find what you need

| Task | Where to start |
|---|---|
| Review conversations and recordings | **Activity** |
| Follow up with a customer | **Requests** |
| Find a customer record | **Customers** |
| Review performance | **Analytics**, when available for your role |
| Work on orders or inventory | The relevant section in the sidebar, when available |
| Update your profile or email alerts | **Settings** |
| Manage your dealership's team and configuration | **Rooftop Settings**, as a rooftop admin |

The sections you see depend on your dealership's enabled features and your assigned role. Ask your rooftop admin if something you need is missing.
