The **Settings** page is accessible to all LotTech Voice users from the sidebar navigation. From here you can update your personal details, change your password, and manage your notification preferences — keeping your profile accurate and your account secure.

<Tip>
  Keep your phone number up to date in your profile so that colleagues can reach you quickly when they need to.
</Tip>

## Updating Your Profile

Your profile stores the personal information associated with your LotTech Voice account. You can update your name and phone number at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the sidebar, click **Settings** to navigate to your profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Edit your **First Name** and **Last Name** as needed — both fields are required. Optionally, enter or update your **Phone Number** in US phone format (e.g., (555) 867-5309).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to apply your updates. Your new profile information will take effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your email address is displayed on the Settings page for reference but cannot be changed here. Email addresses are managed by your administrator through the platform's authentication system. Contact your LotTech administrator if your email address needs to be updated.
</Note>

## Changing Your Password

You can update your password directly from the Settings page at any time, as long as you know your current password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the sidebar, click **Settings** and scroll down to the **Password** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Type your existing password into the **Current Password** field to verify your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a new password">
    Type your new password into the **New Password** field. Your new password must be at least **8 characters** long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your new password">
    Re-enter your new password in the confirmation field to ensure there are no typos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your new password">
    Click **Save**. Your password will be updated immediately. Use your new password the next time you log in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you have forgotten your current password, you will not be able to change it from the Settings page. Instead, log out and use the **Forgot Password?** link on the login page at `/auth/login` to reset your password via email.
</Warning>

## Notification Preferences

LotTech Voice can send you notifications to keep you informed of activity within your assigned rooftops and modules. You can enable or disable these notifications from the Settings page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the sidebar, click **Settings** and locate the **Notifications** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle notifications on or off">
    Use the toggle switch to enable or disable notifications according to your preference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your preference">
    Your notification setting is saved automatically when you toggle the switch.
  </Step>
</Steps>
