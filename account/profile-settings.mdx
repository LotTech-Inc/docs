The **Settings** page is accessible to all LotTech Voice users from the sidebar navigation. From here you can update your personal details, change your password, and manage your notification and voice transfer preferences — keeping your profile accurate and your account secure.

<Tip>
  Keep your phone number up to date in your profile so that colleagues can reach you quickly when they need to.
</Tip>

## Updating Your Profile

Your profile stores the personal information associated with your LotTech Voice account. You can update your name and phone number at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the sidebar, click **Settings** to navigate to your profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Edit your **First Name** and **Last Name** as needed — both fields are required. Optionally, enter or update your **Phone Number** in US phone format (e.g., (555) 867-5309).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes** to apply your updates. Your new profile information will take effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your email address is displayed on the Settings page for reference but cannot be changed here. Email addresses are managed by your administrator. Contact your LotTech administrator if your email address needs to be updated.
</Note>

## Changing Your Password

You can update your password directly from the Settings page at any time, as long as you know your current password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the sidebar, click **Settings** and scroll down to the **Change Password** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Type your existing password into the **Current Password** field to verify your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a new password">
    Type your new password into the **New Password** field. Your new password must be at least **8 characters** long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your new password">
    Re-enter your new password in the confirmation field to ensure there are no typos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your new password">
    Click **Update Password**. Your password will be updated immediately. Use your new password the next time you log in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you have forgotten your current password, you will not be able to change it from the Settings page. Instead, log out and use the **Forgot Password?** link on the login page at `/auth/login` to reset your password via email.
</Warning>

## Notification Preferences

LotTech Voice can send you email notifications to keep you informed of activity within your assigned rooftops and modules. You can enable or disable these notifications from the Settings page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the sidebar, click **Settings** and locate the **Notifications** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle email notifications on or off">
    Use the **Email notifications** toggle to enable or disable email alerts for new requests and customer follow-up activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle voice transfers on or off">
    Use the **Voice transfers** toggle to control whether the AI voice agent can transfer callers directly to your phone when a caller asks for you by name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your preferences">
    Both notification settings are saved automatically when you toggle each switch — no additional save step is required.
  </Step>
</Steps>
