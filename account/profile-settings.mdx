Both managers and rooftop admins can update their own account from **Settings** in the sidebar.

## Update your profile

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Update your first name, last name, or phone number in **Profile**.
3. Select **Save Changes**.

Keep your phone number current if you receive calls transferred to you by name. Your email address is shown for reference and cannot be edited on this page. Contact LotTech support if it needs to change.

## Change your password

1. Open **Settings → Change Password**.
2. Enter your **Current Password**.
3. Enter your **New Password** and repeat it in **Confirm New Password**.
4. Select **Update Password**.

If you have forgotten your current password, use the password reset link on the sign-in page.

## Email notifications

Under **Notifications**, use **Email notifications** to turn request and follow-up alerts on or off. The change saves automatically.

See [Email notifications](/account/email-notifications) for the full guide, including how rooftop admins manage teammates' preferences.

## Voice transfers

Under **Notifications**, use **Voice transfers** to allow or stop transfers to your phone when callers ask for you by name. The change saves automatically.

To stop receiving these transfers, turn off **Voice transfers**. Keep your profile phone number accurate.
