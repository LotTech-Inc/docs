Email notifications alert you when new requests are created or customer follow-up is needed. Managers and rooftop admins can control their own preference. Rooftop admins can also manage the preference for teammates.

## Turn on your own notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Select **Settings** in the dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Notifications">
    Scroll to the **Notifications** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Email notifications">
    Turn on **Email notifications**. The change saves automatically; no separate save button is needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turn the same toggle off to stop email alerts. This does not change your access to requests in the dashboard.

## Manage a teammate's notifications

**For rooftop admins.**

1. Select the dealership you manage.
2. Open **Rooftop Settings → Team**.
3. Find the teammate in the team list.
4. Turn their **Email notifications** toggle on or off. The change saves automatically.

<Note>
  This updates the teammate's personal notification preference, the same setting they control from their own **Settings** page. It is not a separate subscription for this dealership.
</Note>

## Check missing notifications

If someone expects an email and does not receive it:

- Check that **Email notifications** is on.
- Check the email address shown on their profile and their spam or junk folder.
- Ask the rooftop admin to confirm that the person has joined the correct dealership and has the role needed for the request.
- Check **Pending Invites** if they have not finished accepting their invitation.

Enabling email notifications does not grant access to another dealership or department. If the settings look correct, contact LotTech support with the dealership, approximate time, and affected request.
