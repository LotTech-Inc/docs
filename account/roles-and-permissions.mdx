Your role controls what you can do at an assigned dealership. LotTech's customer dashboard has two kinds of roles: **managers** and **rooftop admins**.

## Managers

Managers handle day-to-day customer work. Your role may appear as **Parts Manager**, **Service Manager**, **Cosmetics Manager**, **Commerce Manager**, **Rental Manager**, or **Email Manager**, depending on your responsibilities.

You can hold more than one manager role at a dealership. The sections available to you depend on those roles and the products enabled for that location.

As a manager, you can work in the areas assigned to you and update your own profile, email notifications, and voice transfer preferences. Your rooftop admin manages shared dealership settings and team access.

## Rooftop admins

Rooftop admins manage their assigned dealership's team and configuration. In addition to working in the dealership's enabled areas, you can:

- Invite teammates and assign or remove their rooftop roles.
- Manage teammates' email notification and voice transfer preferences.
- Update dealership contact information, address, and branding.
- Set business hours and holiday closures.
- Configure the receptionist's name, voice, and department transfer numbers.
- Set up available scheduling and payment integrations.

A rooftop admin role applies to the dealership where it is assigned. It does not provide access to other locations automatically.

## Compare access

| Task | Manager | Rooftop admin |
|---|---|---|
| Open assigned rooftops | Yes | Yes |
| Handle customer work | Within assigned manager roles | Across the rooftop's enabled areas |
| Update your own profile and preferences | Yes | Yes |
| Invite teammates and manage rooftop roles | No | Yes |
| Change teammates' notification preferences | No | Yes |
| Edit shared dealership settings | No | Yes |
| Configure available scheduling and payment integrations | No | Yes |

## Missing a page or an edit button?

First, confirm that you have selected the correct dealership. Then ask your rooftop admin to check your role.

Some features also need to be enabled for the dealership. A rooftop admin can contact LotTech support about adding a product, activating a communication channel, or setting up another location.
