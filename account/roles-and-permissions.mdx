LotTech Voice uses a two-role system to control what each user can see and do on the platform. **Admins** have full, cross-rooftop access to every feature and setting, while **Managers** are scoped to the specific rooftops they are assigned to and the modules their administrator has enabled for them.

<Note>
  Only Admins can assign or change a user's role and module permissions. If you need your access updated, contact your LotTech administrator.
</Note>

## Admin Role

Admins have unrestricted access to the entire LotTech Voice platform. After logging in, Admins are taken to the **Admin Home** — a cross-rooftop overview that surfaces activity and data from all locations in the dealership group.

As an Admin, you can:

- **Manage all users** — create new accounts, assign roles, update module permissions, and deactivate users across every rooftop.
- **Create and configure rooftops** — add new dealership locations and adjust rooftop-level settings.
- **Access all modules** — every module (Communications, Reporting, Parts, Commerce, Inventory, and Rental) is available to you on every rooftop without requiring explicit permission grants.
- **View cross-rooftop data** — see customers, interactions, requests, analytics, and inventory across your entire dealer group from a single dashboard.

## Manager Role

Managers have access limited to the rooftops they are assigned to and the modules explicitly enabled for them on each of those rooftops. After logging in, Managers land on their primary rooftop dashboard.

As a Manager, you can:

- **View and work within assigned rooftops** — access customers, interactions, requests, and other module data for the locations you are assigned to.
- **Edit limited rooftop settings** — adjust certain operational settings within your assigned rooftops, subject to what your Admin has permitted.

As a Manager, you **cannot**:

- Create or deactivate user accounts.
- Create or delete rooftops.
- Access rooftops you have not been assigned to.
- Access modules that have not been enabled for you on a given rooftop.

## Module Permissions

Module permissions are granted per rooftop by an Admin. When a module is enabled for you on a rooftop, you gain access to all features within that module for that location. Admins always have access to every module on every rooftop.

| Module | What It Controls | Who Has Access |
|---|---|---|
| **Communications** | Customers, interactions, requests, and call logs | Admins + Managers with permission |
| **Reporting** | Analytics dashboards and performance data | Admins + Managers with permission |
| **Parts** | Organizations (B2B and wholesale buyers) | Admins + Managers with permission |
| **Commerce** | Orders and custom pricing rules | Admins + Managers with permission |
| **Inventory** | Retail vehicle inventory and parts inventory | Admins + Managers with permission |
| **Rental** | Rental fleet inventory and availability | Admins + Managers with permission |

If you navigate to a module and see an access error or the section does not appear in your sidebar, your Admin has not yet enabled that module for you on the relevant rooftop. Reach out to your administrator to request access.

## Rooftop Scope

A **rooftop** represents a single dealership location. Your access to data in LotTech Voice is determined by which rooftops you are assigned to.

- **Managers** can only see and interact with data — customers, interactions, requests, inventory, and so on — that belongs to their assigned rooftops. If your dealership group has multiple locations, you will only see the ones your Admin has explicitly assigned to you.
- **Admins** have visibility across all rooftops in the platform. The Admin Home aggregates data from every location, and Admins can switch between rooftops freely.

If you are a Manager and need access to an additional rooftop, contact your LotTech administrator to update your rooftop assignments.
