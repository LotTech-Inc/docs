LotTech Voice accounts are provisioned by your LotTech administrator or dealership administrator — there is no public self-registration page. Once your administrator adds you to the platform, you will receive an email invitation with everything you need to get started.

<Warning>
  Your account is tied exclusively to your dealership's LotTech Voice instance. Never share your login credentials with anyone, including colleagues. Each team member should have their own account.
</Warning>

## Accepting Your Invitation

When your administrator creates your account, you will receive an invitation email at the address they registered for you. Follow these steps to activate your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation email">
    Check your inbox for an email from LotTech Voice. The subject line will indicate that you have been invited to join your dealership's platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">
    Click the link inside the invitation email. You will be taken to a secure page where you can create a password for your account. Choose a strong password of at least 8 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    After setting your password, LotTech Voice will send a verification email to your address. Open that email and click the verification link to confirm your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in for the first time">
    Once your email is verified, navigate to your dealership's LotTech Voice URL and sign in with your email address and the password you just created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you do not receive a verification email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. If it still does not appear, contact your administrator to resend the verification or confirm that the correct email address was used.
</Note>

## Logging In

After your account is activated, you can log in at any time from your dealership's LotTech Voice login page at `/auth/login`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the login page">
    Go to your dealership's LotTech Voice URL. You will be directed to the login screen automatically if you are not already signed in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type your email address and password into the provided fields, then click **Sign In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your dashboard">
    Upon successful login, you will land on your home screen. Admins are taken to the cross-rooftop Admin Home, while Managers are taken to their assigned rooftop dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you have forgotten your password, click the **Forgot Password?** link on the login page to begin the reset process.

## Forgot Your Password

If you cannot remember your password, you can request a reset link at any time from the login page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Forgot Password page">
    On the login page at `/auth/login`, click the **Forgot Password?** link. You will be taken to the password reset request page at `/auth/forgot-password`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type the email address associated with your LotTech Voice account and click **Send Reset Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the reset email">
    Check your inbox for a password reset email from LotTech Voice. Click the link inside to open the reset form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Enter and confirm your new password — it must be at least 8 characters long. Click **Update Password** to save your credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your new password">
    Return to the login page and sign in using your email address and your newly created password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Password reset links expire after 24 hours for security reasons. If your link has expired, simply return to `/auth/forgot-password` and request a new one.
</Note>
