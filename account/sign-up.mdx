Your rooftop admin can invite you to the dealership's LotTech dashboard. Use the email address that received the invitation.

## Accept a new account invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation">
    Open the link in your invitation email, then select **Accept invitation** on the page that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your account details">
    Enter your first name, last name, phone number, and password. Confirm your password and review the Privacy Notice.

    Use a phone number where you can receive transferred calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to your dashboard">
    Select **Save and continue**. You will be taken to the dashboard, or to the dealership setup steps if your invitation is for a new rooftop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you already have a LotTech account, your rooftop admin can invite your existing email address to grant a role at the dealership. Sign in with your existing account.

## Sign in again

Open your LotTech dashboard and enter your email address and password. After signing in, check the dealership name at the top of the page.

## An invitation is missing or expired

- Check your spam or junk folder and confirm the address with your rooftop admin.
- If the invitation page says the link is no longer valid, use its option to request a fresh link.
- Your rooftop admin can also select **Resend** from **Rooftop Settings → Team → Pending Invites**.
- Use the newest invitation email if several links have been sent.

## Reset your password

Use the password reset link on the sign-in page, enter your account email, and follow the instructions in the reset email. If the link has expired, request a new one.

Once signed in, you can change your password from [Settings](/account/profile-settings).
