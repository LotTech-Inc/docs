Every time your AI voice agent handles a call, it automatically creates **requests** for any tasks that need your team's follow-up — a quote, a parts check, a scheduled appointment. The Requests page gives you a single place to track, assign, and close out every open task so nothing gets missed between your AI agent and your service team.

## What Is a Request?

A request is a task the AI voice agent opened on behalf of a customer during an interaction. Requests capture what the customer asked for and route that task to the right person on your team.

Each request belongs to one of five types:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Service" icon="wrench">
    Repair, maintenance, or diagnostic work — the most common request type.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Parts" icon="gears">
    A parts inquiry, availability check, or order.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cosmetics" icon="paint-roller">
    Body work, detailing, paint, or appearance-related jobs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rental" icon="car">
    Loaner or rental vehicle requests tied to a service visit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="General" icon="circle-info">
    Anything that doesn't fit the above categories — directions, hours, pricing questions, etc.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Requests Dashboard

Navigate to **Requests** in the left sidebar to open the full requests list. At the top of the page, a stats strip gives you an instant snapshot of your workload:

| Stat | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Total** | All requests across every status |
| **Pending** | Open tasks that still need action |
| **Fulfilled** | Completed requests |
| **Cancelled** | Requests the customer withdrew or your team cancelled |

## Request Statuses

Every request moves through a defined lifecycle. Use the status to communicate where each task stands:

| Status | Meaning | Typical next action |
|---|---|---|
| `pending` | The request is open and waiting for your team to act on it. | Assign to a team member and begin follow-up. |
| `fulfilled` | Your team completed the request. | No action needed — archive for reference. |
| `cancelled` | The customer cancelled, or your team withdrew the request. | Log a reason in the notes if helpful. |
| `denied` | The request cannot be fulfilled (part unavailable, policy, etc.). | Notify the customer and document why. |

## Filtering Requests

Use the filter controls at the top of the Requests list to zero in on what matters right now:

- **Status** — pending, fulfilled, cancelled, or denied
- **Type** — service, parts, cosmetics, rental, or general
- **Date range** — limit results to a specific time window
- **Assigned user** — see only requests belonging to a team member
- **Search** — type a customer name, phone number, or keyword to find a specific request

Filters can be combined. For example, filter by `pending` + `parts` + the current week to see all open parts requests created this week.

## Viewing Request Details

Click any request in the list to open its detail page. You'll see:

- **Request type and status**
- **Customer name and phone number**
- **Rooftop** the request belongs to
- **Assigned team member**
- **Creation timestamp**
- **Request notes** — what the AI captured from the conversation
- **Associated interaction** — a link back to the original call or SMS

## Fulfilling a Request

When your team completes a request, mark it fulfilled directly from the detail page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    Click the request from the Requests list or from the Needs Attention card on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the details">
    Confirm the customer, type, and notes match the work completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the status">
    Select **Fulfilled** from the status dropdown (or **Cancelled** / **Denied** as appropriate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes (optional)">
    Enter any relevant follow-up notes — part number ordered, appointment time confirmed, etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** to commit the status change. The request will move out of your pending queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning Requests

Assigning a request to a specific team member routes ownership clearly and ensures the right person is responsible for follow-up.

From the request detail page, click the **Assigned To** field and select a team member from the dropdown. The assignee will appear in the list view and in any filtered views for that user.

<Tip>
  Use assignment together with the **Assigned User** filter to run a daily standup view — filter by your name to see everything currently in your queue.
</Tip>

## Attention Queue

The home dashboard surfaces the **5 oldest pending requests** in the **Needs Attention** card. These are sorted oldest-first so the most overdue tasks rise to the top.

Use the Needs Attention card as your starting point each shift — clear those five before digging into the full Requests list.

<Note>
  **Bulk delete** is available to users with the Admin role. From the Requests list, select multiple requests using the checkboxes and choose **Delete Selected** from the bulk actions menu. Use this to remove test records or clean up cancelled requests.
</Note>
