Every time your AI voice agent connects with a customer — whether by phone or text — LotTech Voice captures the full event as an **interaction**. From a single interaction detail page you can review the transcript, see what actions the agent took, check which requests were created, and leave notes for your team.

## What Is an Interaction?

An interaction is a recorded contact event between your AI voice agent (or a live agent) and a customer. Each interaction captures everything relevant to that touchpoint:

- **Channel** — `voice` (phone call) or `sms` (text message)
- **Direction** — inbound (customer reached out) or outbound (your team or AI initiated contact)
- **Duration** — total call length (voice calls only)
- **Start and end time** — when the contact event began and ended
- **Caller identity** — customer name and phone number
- **Requests created** — tasks the AI agent opened during the interaction
- **Actions taken** — e.g., appointment booked, parts order submitted
- **Transfer status** — whether the call was escalated to a human agent

## Browsing Interactions

Your full interaction history lives under **Activity** in the left sidebar. Navigate there to see a chronological list of all inbound and outbound contacts across voice and SMS channels.

<Tip>
  A **Recent Activity** strip on your home dashboard also surfaces the latest interactions at a glance — no need to navigate away for a quick check.
</Tip>

You can also reach a customer's interactions directly from their profile: go to **Dashboard → Customers**, select a customer, and open the **Interactions** tab on their profile page.

## Interaction Details

Click any interaction in the list to open its detail page. Here you'll find everything captured during that contact event:

## Caller ID information

The caller ID badge describes the phone number shown for a call:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | The call's number passed the caller ID check. |
| **Unconfirmed** | The caller's number could not be confirmed. |
| **Caller ID hidden** | The displayed source would be a dealership routing number, so it is hidden. |
| **Unavailable** | No caller phone number is available. |

A number check does not verify the caller's identity. Review the conversation and confirm contact details before following up.

## Adding Notes

Each interaction detail page includes a **Notes** field where you can record internal context about that contact — what was discussed, what follow-up is needed, or any relevant details your team should know.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the interaction">
    Navigate to **Activity** and click the interaction you want to annotate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Notes field">
    Find the **Notes** section on the detail page and click into the text area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type and save your note">
    Enter your note. Changes save automatically when you click away from the field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Notes are visible to all team members with access to that rooftop and are never shared with the customer.

## Adding Comments

In addition to notes, you can post threaded **comments** on any interaction from the bottom of the detail page. Comments are useful for team discussion and handoff — each one is timestamped and attributed to the person who posted it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the interaction">
    Navigate to **Activity** and click the interaction you want to discuss.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Comments">
    Scroll to the **Comments** section at the bottom of the detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post your comment">
    Click the comment field, enter your message, and press **Add Comment**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Comments give you a clear audit trail for each interaction and are never visible to the customer.

## Filtering Activity

On the Activity page, use the filter controls in the toolbar to narrow down interactions:

- **Channel** — show only `voice` or `sms` interactions
- **Direction** — filter by inbound or outbound
- **Date range** — pick a start and end date to scope the list

Filters stack, so you can combine them — for example, show only inbound voice calls from the past seven days.

<Tip>
  Use the **Back to Activity** button from an interaction detail page to return to your previous view without losing your filter state.
</Tip>
