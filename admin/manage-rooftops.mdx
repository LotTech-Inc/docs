The Manage Rooftops page gives you a bird's-eye view of every dealership location running on your LotTech Voice platform. From here you can see which rooftops are active, create new locations, jump directly into any rooftop's settings, and manage archived locations. Only users with the **Admin** role can access this page.

## Accessing Rooftop Management

Navigate to **Admin** in the left sidebar, then select **Rooftops**. The full URL is `/dashboard/admin/rooftops`. If the Admin section is not visible in your sidebar, your account does not hold the Admin role.

## Viewing Rooftops

The Rooftops page lists every location in your platform. Each entry shows:

- **Rooftop name** and address
- **Phone number(s)** assigned to the location
- **Assigned managers** — the team members with access
- **Active modules** — which products (Parts, Cosmetics, Service, Rental) are enabled

To see rooftops that have been deactivated, toggle **Show Deleted** at the top of the list. Deleted rooftops appear with a visual indicator and are otherwise read-only unless you restore them.

## Creating a New Rooftop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the onboarding form">
    Click **Create Rooftop** at the top right of the Rooftops page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the Rooftop Onboarding Form">
    Enter the dealership name, full address, primary phone number, and select which modules to enable for this location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the form">
    Click **Submit**. The new rooftop appears in the list immediately and is ready for further configuration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you have a dealer setup document on hand, use the **Onboard from PDF** feature in Admin &gt; Onboarding instead. It parses your document automatically and pre-fills the rooftop form — saving significant manual entry time, especially when setting up multiple locations at once.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Creating a rooftop does not automatically assign it a phone number. After the rooftop is created, navigate to its settings page and open the **Overview** tab to configure phone numbers and complete the initial setup.
</Note>

## Opening Rooftop Settings

Click on any rooftop in the list to navigate directly to that location's settings page. All configuration tabs are accessible from there:

- **Overview** — name, address, phone numbers, and basic configuration
- **Operations** — business hours, routing rules, and escalation behavior
- **Commerce** — module-specific settings for Parts, Cosmetics, Service, and Rental
- **Team** — managers assigned to this rooftop

## Deactivating a Rooftop

Deactivating (archiving) a rooftop immediately stops it from receiving calls or SMS messages. It is hidden from the main Rooftops list but remains accessible via the **Show Deleted** toggle. Deactivated rooftops retain all their historical data and settings.

If you need to permanently remove a rooftop and delete its data, contact **LotTech Support** — permanent deletion cannot be performed from within the platform.
