The Manage Rooftops page gives you a bird's-eye view of every dealership location running on your LotTech Voice platform. From here you can see which rooftops are active, add new locations, jump directly into any rooftop's settings, and manage archived locations. Only users with the **Admin** role can access this page.

## Accessing Rooftop Management

Navigate to **Admin** in the left sidebar, then select **Rooftops**. If the Admin section is not visible in your sidebar, your account does not hold the Admin role.

## Viewing and Searching Rooftops

The Rooftops page lists every location in your platform. Each entry shows:

- **Rooftop name** and URL slug
- **Contact information** — AI agent name, voice phone number, and SMS phone number if configured separately
- **Active products** — which modules (Parts, Cosmetics, Service, Commerce, Rental, Email) are enabled on the rooftop
- **Last activity** — when the rooftop configuration was last updated

Use the **search bar** to find a specific rooftop by name or slug. To see rooftops that have been deactivated, enable the **Show Deleted** toggle. Deleted rooftops appear with a visual indicator.

## Filtering by Product

Use the filter dropdown to narrow the list to rooftops that have a specific product enabled. Options include Has Parts, Has Cosmetics, Has Service, Has Commerce, Has Rental, All Products (every module enabled), and No Products (no modules active).

## Adding a New Rooftop

There are two ways to add a rooftop from this page:

- **Add New Rooftop** — opens the step-by-step Rooftop Onboarding wizard for manual entry.
- **Add from PDF** — opens the PDF onboarding flow to create a rooftop (and staff accounts) from an uploaded dealer setup document.

<Tip>
  If you have a dealer setup document on hand, use **Add from PDF** instead of filling out the wizard manually. The PDF flow parses your document, pre-fills the rooftop fields, and also creates staff user accounts in the same workflow — saving significant manual entry time.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Creating a rooftop does not automatically assign it a phone number. After the rooftop is created, navigate to its settings page to configure voice and SMS phone numbers and complete the initial setup.
</Note>

## Opening Rooftop Settings

Click on any rooftop in the list to navigate directly to that location's settings page, where you can configure all aspects of that rooftop.

## Deactivating a Rooftop

Deleting (archiving) a rooftop stops it from receiving calls or SMS messages. It is hidden from the main Rooftops list but remains accessible via the **Show Deleted** toggle. Deactivated rooftops retain all their historical data and settings.

<Warning>
  If you need to permanently remove a rooftop and delete its data, contact **LotTech Support** — permanent deletion cannot be performed from within the platform.
</Warning>
