Connecting DealerFX to LotTech Voice gives your AI agent direct access to your dealership's DealerFX scheduling system. When a customer calls to book a service visit, the AI checks real-time availability in DealerFX and confirms the appointment during the call — keeping your schedule accurate and eliminating the need for manual callback to book.

## What DealerFX Integration Does

With DealerFX connected, your AI voice agent can:

- **Access live appointment availability** from your DealerFX system in real time during a call
- **Offer open service time slots** to the caller and confirm a booking without escalating to a service advisor
- **Write the confirmed appointment to DealerFX** so it appears in your existing scheduling workflow alongside all other bookings

Without DealerFX (or another scheduling integration), the AI will still collect service appointment requests from callers and save them as pending items in your LotTech dashboard for your team to book manually.

## Setting Up DealerFX

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rooftop Settings">
    Navigate to **Rooftop Settings** from the main navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Operations tab">
    Select the **Operations** tab within Rooftop Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the DealerFX section and click Setup">
    Locate the **DealerFX** section on the Operations page and click **Setup** to open the DealerFX configuration modal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your DealerFX credentials">
    In the Setup modal, enter the integration credentials provided by your DealerFX account manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your credentials">
    Click **Save** to apply the configuration. The AI agent will use these credentials to authenticate with DealerFX on every scheduling request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Contact your **DealerFX account manager** to obtain the integration credentials required for this setup. LotTech support cannot provide DealerFX credentials on your behalf.
</Note>

## How It Works During Calls

When a caller requests a service appointment, the AI agent manages the booking end-to-end without a transfer:

1. The AI collects the caller's vehicle information and preferred date or time range.
2. LotTech Voice queries DealerFX in real time for open service slots matching the request.
3. The AI presents available times to the caller and confirms their selection.
4. The appointment is written to DealerFX and the caller receives an SMS confirmation.

The confirmed appointment appears in DealerFX immediately, visible to your service team alongside all other scheduled work.

## Disconnecting DealerFX

To remove your DealerFX integration:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rooftop Settings → Operations">
    Navigate to **Rooftop Settings** and select the **Operations** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the DealerFX Setup modal">
    Click **Setup** in the DealerFX section to open the configuration modal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove your credentials and save">
    Clear your credentials from the fields and click **Save** to disconnect the integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After disconnecting, the AI will revert to capturing service appointment requests as pending items in your LotTech dashboard for your team to book manually through DealerFX or another tool.

<Tip>
  **xTime** and **DealerFX** serve the same purpose in LotTech Voice — real-time service scheduling. Only one needs to be active at a time. Choose the integration that matches your dealer management system, and leave the other unconfigured. See [xTime](/integrations/xtime) if your dealership uses xTime instead.
</Tip>
