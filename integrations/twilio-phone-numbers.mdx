LotTech Voice uses Twilio to provision and manage the phone numbers your dealership publishes to customers. Every inbound call to one of these numbers is answered by your AI voice agent, ensuring no call is missed and every caller is greeted immediately.

## How Phone Numbers Work

Each rooftop is assigned one or more **Twilio DIDs** (Direct Inward Dialing numbers) — these are real, dialable phone numbers that you can advertise on your website, in ads, and on service reminders. When a customer dials your DID, Twilio routes the call directly to your LotTech Voice AI agent.

The AI agent answers, handles the call, and — when needed — transfers the caller to a human using the department phone numbers you configure. Your Twilio DIDs are the entry point for all AI-handled inbound calls.

<Note>
  Phone numbers are assigned per rooftop. The same Twilio number cannot ring into multiple rooftops simultaneously.
</Note>

## Viewing Your Phone Numbers

To see all phone numbers currently assigned to your rooftop:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rooftop Settings">
    Navigate to **Rooftop Settings** from the main navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Overview tab">
    Select the **Overview** tab within Rooftop Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Owned DIDs section">
    Scroll to the **Owned DIDs** section to see a list of all Twilio numbers assigned to your rooftop, along with their labels and status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Inbound call routing begins working as soon as a DID is assigned to your rooftop and your rooftop has an active voice configuration. No additional steps are required to activate a new number.
</Info>

## Department Phone Numbers

In addition to your inbound DIDs, you can configure department-specific phone numbers that the AI agent uses as **transfer targets** when escalating a call to a human. These are the internal or direct numbers your staff actually answers.

| Department | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Service line** | Receives transferred calls from service-related requests |
| **Parts line** | Receives transferred calls from parts-related requests |

To set or update these numbers, go to **Rooftop Settings → Overview** and enter the correct phone number for each department. Keep these numbers current so escalated callers always reach the right person.

## Adding or Removing Numbers

Phone number provisioning — adding new DIDs to your rooftop or releasing numbers you no longer need — is managed by your **LotTech administrator**. You cannot provision or remove Twilio numbers directly from the Rooftop Settings panel.

To request a new number or release an existing one, contact LotTech support and provide:

- The rooftop name and ID
- Whether you need to add or remove a number
- The intended use for any new number (for example, a dedicated service line)
