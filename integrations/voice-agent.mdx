Your LotTech receptionist answers calls routed to it and helps customers with the services enabled at your dealership.

## What your team sees

After a conversation, review **Activity** for the details and any available transcript or recording. Open related **Requests** when a customer needs follow-up from your team.

Depending on the customer's needs and your dealership's setup, the receptionist can gather request details, discuss available appointments, or transfer the call to your team. A general question may be answered without creating follow-up work.

## Requests and appointments

For requests that need a person, review the customer's details before contacting them. Keep assignments and status up to date so your teammates can see who is handling the work.

If a scheduling integration is connected, check the appointment details in your scheduling system. A request for a callback is different from a confirmed appointment.

## Call transfers

Rooftop admins configure department transfer numbers in **Rooftop Settings → Overview**. Each teammate controls their own **Voice transfers** preference in **Settings**, and rooftop admins can manage it from the **Team** tab.

See [Phone numbers and transfers](/rooftop/phone-and-transfers) for steps.

## After-hours information

Keep [business hours and holiday closures](/rooftop/business-hours) current so the receptionist can explain when your dealership is open. Your team can review follow-up work in the dashboard when they return.

## Change the receptionist's voice

Rooftop admins can choose and preview a voice, adjust its speed, and set pronunciations. See [Receptionist voice](/rooftop/configure-voice).
