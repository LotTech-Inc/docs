LotTech Voice connects to Stripe to power payment processing on your dealership's customer-facing parts storefront. Once your Stripe account is active, customers can pay for parts orders online, payouts flow directly to your connected bank account, and order revenue is tracked in your LotTech dashboard.

Stripe is configured per rooftop under **Rooftop Settings → Commerce**. Setup is completed through Stripe's hosted onboarding flow, which walks you through account verification, banking details, and tax configuration.

## Stripe Connection Status

Your Stripe connection status is visible at any time in **Rooftop Settings → Commerce**. There are three possible states:

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Not Connected** | No Stripe account has been linked to this rooftop. Online payments are unavailable. |
| **Pending** | A Stripe account has been started but onboarding is not yet complete. Payments cannot be processed until onboarding is finished. |
| **Active** | Stripe onboarding is complete and payments are enabled. Customers can check out on the storefront. |

If your status shows **Pending**, return to **Rooftop Settings → Commerce** and click **Resume Setup** to complete the remaining onboarding steps in Stripe's dashboard.

## What Stripe Enables

Once your Stripe connection is **Active**, the following features are unlocked across your LotTech platform:

- **Customer checkout** — shoppers on your parts storefront can pay by credit or debit card at checkout
- **Revenue tracking** — completed order totals appear in the Orders module of your LotTech dashboard
- **Automatic payouts** — Stripe deposits collected funds to your connected bank account on your payout schedule
- **Tax collection** — after activation, you confirm your tax settings so the correct tax rates are applied to orders

<Info>
  Stripe is required to accept online payments through the Commerce module. You can still manage your parts inventory and view orders in LotTech without a Stripe connection, but customers will not be able to complete purchases on the storefront until Stripe is active.
</Info>
