Follow these steps when you first join your dealership's LotTech dashboard. They apply to both managers and rooftop admins.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation">
    Open the invitation sent to your email address and select **Accept invitation**. Complete the account setup form, then select **Save and continue**.

    If you already have an account, sign in with your existing email and password. See [Sign in and invitations](/account/sign-up) for help.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your dealership">
    Confirm the dealership name at the top of the dashboard. If you have access to several locations, use the dealership selector to choose where you want to work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your profile and notifications">
    Open **Settings**. Confirm your name and phone number, then select **Save Changes** if you edit them.

    Under **Notifications**, turn on **Email notifications** if you want alerts for new requests and customer follow-up. Set **Voice transfers** to control whether callers can be transferred to your phone when they ask for you by name. These toggles save automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review customer activity">
    Open **Activity** to review customer conversations. Open a conversation to read the details and review any available transcript or recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up on requests">
    Open **Requests** to find work that needs attention. Review the customer's details and request history before contacting them, then keep the request's assignment and status up to date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your sidebar depends on your role and the features enabled at your dealership. If a section is missing, ask your rooftop admin to check your access.
</Note>

## If you are a rooftop admin

After checking your own account, review:

1. [Your team](/rooftop/team-management) — invite teammates and assign the roles they need.
2. [Email notifications](/account/email-notifications) — check who will receive alerts.
3. [Business hours and closures](/rooftop/business-hours) — confirm the information callers will hear.
4. [Phone numbers and transfers](/rooftop/phone-and-transfers) — confirm where calls should go.
5. [Your receptionist's voice](/rooftop/configure-voice) — preview and save the settings for your dealership.

If you received an invitation to finish setting up a new dealership, follow [Dealership setup](/rooftop/setup).
