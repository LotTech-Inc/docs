This guide walks you through everything from your first login to your first AI-handled call. By the end, you'll have your rooftop configured, your voice agent live on your dealership's phone number, and a clear picture of how interactions flow into your dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Navigate to your LotTech Voice dashboard URL in your browser. Enter your email address and password on the login page, then click **Sign In**.

    If you haven't received your credentials yet, contact your LotTech administrator or your LotTech representative. They'll provision your account and send you a welcome email with a link to set your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your rooftop">
    After a successful login, the dashboard automatically redirects you based on your role. Admins land on the cross-rooftop **Admin** dashboard at `/dashboard/admin`. All other users are taken directly to their assigned rooftop's dashboard.

    If you manage multiple rooftop locations, use the **rooftop switcher** in the left sidebar to change locations. All data you see — interactions, requests, inventory, orders — belongs to the rooftop currently selected. Switch rooftops at any time without logging out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your dashboard">
    The home page gives you an at-a-glance view of what's happening at your rooftop right now:

    - **Analytics** — key performance metrics for your rooftop, including total interactions, requests created, and average fulfillment time. Visible to users with the Reporting module enabled.
    - **Needs attention** — your oldest pending requests that have not yet been assigned or resolved, sorted with the most overdue at the top.
    - **My requests** — pending requests assigned specifically to you.
    - **Recent activity** — the last 8 interactions handled by the AI voice agent.

    Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the layout before moving on to configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your voice agent">
    Go to **Rooftop Settings > Overview** to set up the core details your voice agent needs to handle calls correctly:

    1. **Phone number** — Connect the Twilio phone number customers will call. This is the number the AI agent monitors for inbound calls.
    2. **AI voice** — Choose a voice from the Rime or Cartesia TTS providers to match the tone of your dealership.
    3. **Business hours** — Define when your dealership is open so the agent can greet callers appropriately and manage after-hours expectations.

    Save your settings before moving to the next step. Changes take effect immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle your first call">
    With your rooftop configured, your AI voice agent is live. Call the Twilio number you connected in the previous step to hear the agent in action.

    After the call ends, return to your dashboard. You'll see:

    - A new entry in **Interactions** with a full transcript and recording
    - Any **requests** the agent captured during the call
    - An updated entry in **Call Logs**

    Your team can now review, assign, and resolve requests directly from the dashboard — no manual call notes required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Admins can create additional rooftops and invite new users from the **Admin** panel. If you need to add a team member or set up a second location, head to **Admin > Manage Users** or **Admin > Rooftops**.
</Note>
