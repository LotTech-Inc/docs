The Analytics page gives you a real-time window into how your AI voice agent is performing — from call volume and request creation to fulfillment speed and AI action breakdowns. Use it to spot trends, measure response times, and make informed decisions about staffing and operations across your dealership.

## Accessing Analytics

Navigate to **Analytics** in the left sidebar to open the analytics dashboard. Access requires the `reporting` module permission on your account. If you don't see the Analytics link, contact your Admin to have the permission enabled.

<Note>
  Admins always have access to analytics regardless of module permissions. If you hold the Admin role, you can also view cross-rooftop data from the Admin section.
</Note>

## Time Windows

Use the time window toggle at the top of the Analytics page to choose the reporting period you want to examine.

| Option | Range |
|---|---|
| **Last 24 Hours** | Rolling 24-hour window from the current time |
| **Last 7 Days** | Rolling 7-day window from the current date |
| **Last 30 Days** | Rolling 30-day window from the current date |

All metrics and charts on the page update immediately when you switch time windows.

## Product Filter

Use the product filter to narrow analytics down to a specific department. Select from **Parts**, **Cosmetics**, **Service**, or **Rental** — or leave it set to **All** to see combined data across every active module on your rooftop. The filter applies to the volume chart, KPI tiles, and action breakdowns simultaneously.

## Key Metrics

The four KPI tiles at the top of the Analytics page give you an at-a-glance summary of performance in the selected time window.

- **Interactions** — the total number of AI-handled contacts (calls or SMS messages) in the window. Use this to gauge overall demand on your AI agent.
- **Requests Created** — the total number of service or product requests generated by the AI during those interactions. A high ratio of requests to interactions indicates the AI is effectively capturing customer intent.
- **Average Time to Fulfill** — the median (p50) time from when a request is created to when a team member marks it fulfilled. This reflects how quickly your staff is responding to AI-generated leads.
- **Average Call Duration** *(voice-enabled rooftops)* — the median duration of AI-handled calls in the window. For rooftops without voice enabled, this tile shows **Pending Now** — a live count of requests currently awaiting fulfillment.

## Period-Over-Period Comparison

Every KPI tile displays a delta pill showing how the current window compares to the prior equal-length window. For example, if you are viewing the last 7 days, the pill compares that period against the 7 days before it.

- **Green pill** — performance improved (more interactions, more requests, faster fulfillment)
- **Red pill** — performance declined relative to the prior period

For Average Time to Fulfill, the direction is inverted: a lower number is better, so a decrease shows as green.

## Dashboard Strip

If you have the `reporting` permission, a condensed analytics strip appears directly on your home dashboard. It surfaces your top KPIs at a glance — no need to navigate to the full Analytics page for a quick status check. Click any metric in the strip to jump straight to the Analytics page with the corresponding filter pre-applied.
