The Analytics page gives you a real-time window into how your AI voice agent is performing — from call and SMS volume and request creation to fulfillment speed and AI action breakdowns. Use it to spot trends, measure response times, and make informed decisions about staffing and operations across your dealership.

## Accessing Analytics

Navigate to **Analytics** in the left sidebar to open the analytics dashboard. If you don't see the Analytics link, contact your Admin to verify that the reporting module is enabled on your account.

<Note>
  Admins always have access to analytics. If you hold the Admin role, you can also view cross-rooftop data from the Admin section.
</Note>

## Time Windows

Use the time window toggle at the top of the Analytics page to choose the reporting period you want to examine.

| Option | Range |
|---|---|
| **Day** | Rolling 24-hour window from the current time |
| **Week** | Rolling 7-day window from the current date |
| **Month** | Rolling 30-day window from the current date |

All metrics and charts on the page update immediately when you switch time windows.

## Product Filter

Use the product filter to narrow analytics down to a specific department. Select from **Parts**, **Cosmetics**, **Service**, or **Rental** — or leave it set to **All** to see combined data across every active module on your rooftop. The filter applies to the volume chart, performance metrics, and action breakdowns simultaneously.

<Note>
  Only the products that are enabled on your rooftop appear as filter options. A **General** filter is also available for interactions and requests that are not associated with a specific product.
</Note>

## Page Sections

The Analytics page is organized into four sections, each scoped to the time window and product filter you have selected.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Volume Chart" icon="chart-line">
    Plots interaction or request counts over time with a metric toggle to switch between Interactions, Requests, and Fulfilled views.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Performance" icon="gauge">
    Shows request flow metrics including requests created, average time to fulfill, fulfillment count, time to first action, and pending requests now.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Actions" icon="bolt">
    Breaks down the specific actions your AI took during interactions — appointment bookings, call transfers, and more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="All-Time Totals" icon="infinity">
    Displays cumulative interaction and request counts not scoped to any time window, so you can see the full history at a glance.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Period-Over-Period Comparison

Performance metric tiles display a delta pill showing how the current window compares to the prior equal-length window. For example, if you are viewing the last 7 days, the pill compares that period against the 7 days before it.

- **Green pill** — performance improved (more interactions, more requests, faster fulfillment)
- **Red pill** — performance declined relative to the prior period

For Average Time to Fulfill, the direction is inverted: a lower number is better, so a decrease shows as green.
