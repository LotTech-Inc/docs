When your AI agent handles a customer contact, it creates a structured request that your team needs to act on — a parts quote to return, a service appointment to confirm, or a rental reservation to process. Request metrics tell you how many of those requests are being created, how fast your team is closing them, and how many are still waiting. Use these numbers to manage workload, coach your staff, and keep customers from falling through the cracks.

## Requests Created

The **Requests Created** metric shows the total number of requests generated by the AI in the selected time window. Each request represents a concrete follow-up task for your team — so this number is a direct measure of the AI's output and your team's incoming workload.

The delta pill next to the metric compares requests created in the current window to the prior equal-length window. An increase over the prior period means the AI handled more customer demand; a decrease may indicate lower call volume or a shift in the types of interactions occurring.

## Average Time to Fulfill

**Average Time to Fulfill** measures the median time between when a request is created and when a team member marks it fulfilled. It reflects how responsive your team is to AI-generated leads.

Lower is better — the delta pill for this metric is **inverted**: a decrease in fulfillment time shows as green, and an increase shows as red. If your average fulfillment time is climbing, that is a signal to investigate whether requests are being triaged promptly or sitting unnoticed in the queue.

<Note>
  The Performance section also shows a **90% fulfilled within** metric, which tells you the fulfillment time that covers 90% of your requests. Use this alongside the median to understand how your slowest-to-fulfill requests compare to typical turnaround.
</Note>

## Pending Now

**Pending Now** is a live snapshot of how many requests currently have an open status on your rooftop. Use this as your real-time workload indicator. If the number is unusually high relative to your typical volume, your team may need to reprioritize to clear the backlog before requests age further.

## Pending Aging Breakdown

The Performance section includes a **Currently pending requests** breakdown that groups your open requests by how long they have been waiting:

| Age | Bucket |
|---|---|
| Under 1 day | Fresh requests, typically normal |
| 1–3 days | May need attention soon |
| 3–7 days | Likely overdue; review with your team |
| 7–14 days | Significant delay; escalate |
| 14+ days | Critical; customer follow-up likely needed |

Review this breakdown regularly to catch aging requests before customers follow up on their own.

## Fulfillment Rate

To see how many requests were resolved in a given period, switch the volume chart to the **Fulfilled** metric using the toggle above the chart. Compare this view side-by-side with the Requests view to understand your team's closure rate.

If fulfilled requests are tracking close to requests created, your team is keeping up with demand. A widening gap between the two — more created than fulfilled — signals a growing backlog that will eventually show up as an elevated Pending Now count.

## Using Request Metrics for Coaching

Request metrics are most valuable when you use them to have specific, data-backed conversations with your team. Here are two common patterns to watch for:

- **High Pending Now count** — a large number of open requests suggests your team needs to spend dedicated time working through the queue. Consider adjusting shifts, redistributing assignments, or reviewing whether requests are being acknowledged in a timely way.
- **High Average Time to Fulfill** — a long median fulfillment time often points to a triage problem: requests are being seen but not acted on quickly. Walk through the oldest open requests with your team to identify where delays are happening and whether any process changes would help.

Use the product filter on the Analytics page to isolate these metrics by department — Parts, Cosmetics, Service, or Rental — so you can pinpoint which team or workflow needs attention without noise from the others.

<Tip>
  Use the **Fulfillment** breakdown in the Performance section to see which team members are resolving the most requests in the selected window. This makes it easy to recognize strong performers and to identify who may need additional support.
</Tip>
