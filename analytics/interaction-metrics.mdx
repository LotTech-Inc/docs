Interaction metrics show you exactly how much work your AI agent is handling — how many contacts it fielded, which channels those came through, how long calls ran, and what actions it took on your team's behalf. Use these numbers to understand demand patterns, evaluate AI performance, and identify peak periods that may need extra attention.

## What Counts as an Interaction?

An interaction is any AI-handled contact event — either a phone call answered by your AI voice agent or an SMS message processed by the AI. Each contact event counts as one interaction, regardless of how long the conversation lasts or how many messages it involves. Interactions that are transferred to a human agent mid-conversation still count as a single AI-handled interaction.

## Volume Chart

The volume chart at the top of the Analytics page plots your interaction, request, or fulfillment count over the selected time window. The chart automatically adjusts its resolution based on the window you choose:

- **Day** — data is plotted in hourly buckets
- **Week** — data is plotted in daily buckets
- **Month** — data is plotted in daily buckets

Hover over any point or bar on the chart to see the exact count for that time bucket. Use the product filter to isolate volume for a specific department.

## Switching Metrics

The volume chart is not limited to interactions alone. Use the metric toggle above the chart to switch between three views:

- **Interactions** — total AI-handled contact events
- **Requests** — requests generated by the AI during those interactions
- **Fulfilled** — requests that were marked fulfilled within the window

Switching between these views lets you compare demand (interactions) against output (requests created) and resolution (fulfilled) on the same chart.

## Channel Breakdown

Interactions are categorized by the channel through which the customer reached your AI — either a **voice call** or an **SMS message**. The all-time totals section at the bottom of the Analytics page shows your cumulative voice and SMS counts separately, so you can see which channel drives the most volume over time.

Use the channel breakdown to understand whether your customers prefer calling or texting, and to make sure your AI is configured appropriately for the channels that see the most volume.

## AI Actions

The Analytics page includes an **Actions** section that breaks down the specific actions your AI took during interactions in the selected time window. Actions are grouped by type, and each tile shows the count for the current period along with a delta pill comparing it to the prior equal-length window.

This breakdown tells you not just how many interactions occurred, but what outcomes the AI delivered during them. Review this section regularly to confirm the AI is taking the types of actions you expect for your enabled products.

## Average Call Duration

For voice-enabled rooftops, the **Average Call Duration** metric reflects the median length of AI-handled calls in the selected time window. A shorter average duration is not inherently better — the right length depends on your call types. Use this metric alongside the Actions breakdown to understand whether calls are ending because customers got what they needed or because they dropped off early.

<Tip>
  Use the **Week** view to spot week-over-week trends and identify which days drive the most interaction volume. Switch to **Month** for monthly reporting — it gives you a clean 30-day picture that aligns well with typical performance review cycles.
</Tip>
