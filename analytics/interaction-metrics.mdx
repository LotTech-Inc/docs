Interaction metrics show you exactly how much work your AI agent is handling — how many contacts it fielded, which channels those came through, how long calls ran, and what actions it took on your team's behalf. Use these numbers to understand demand patterns, evaluate AI performance, and identify peak periods that may need extra attention.

## What Counts as an Interaction?

An interaction is any AI-handled contact event — either a phone call answered by your AI voice agent or an SMS message processed by the AI. Each contact event counts as one interaction, regardless of how long the conversation lasts or how many messages it involves. Interactions that are transferred to a human agent mid-conversation still count as a single AI-handled interaction.

## Volume Chart

The volume chart at the center of the Analytics page plots your interaction count over the selected time window. The chart automatically adjusts its resolution based on the window you choose:

- **Last 24 Hours** — data is plotted in hourly buckets
- **Last 7 Days** — data is plotted in daily buckets
- **Last 30 Days** — data is plotted in daily buckets

Hover over any point or bar on the chart to see the exact count for that time bucket. Use the product filter to isolate interaction volume for a specific department.

## Switching Metrics

The volume chart is not limited to interactions alone. Use the metric toggle above the chart to switch between three views:

- **Interactions** — total AI-handled contact events
- **Requests Created** — requests generated by the AI during those interactions
- **Fulfilled Requests** — requests that were marked fulfilled within the window

Switching between these views lets you compare demand (interactions) against output (requests created) and resolution (requests fulfilled) on the same chart.

## Channel Breakdown

Interactions are categorized by the channel through which the customer reached your AI — either a **voice call** or an **SMS message**. The channel for each interaction is displayed on its card in the Activity feed, so you can drill into individual contacts directly from there.

Use the channel breakdown to understand whether your customers prefer calling or texting, and to make sure your AI is configured appropriately for the channels that see the most volume.

## AI Actions

The Analytics page includes a breakdown of the specific actions your AI took during interactions in the selected window. Actions are grouped by type and may include entries such as:

- Appointment booked
- Parts availability checked
- Service quote provided
- Rental reservation created
- Customer directed to staff

This breakdown tells you not just how many interactions occurred, but what value the AI delivered during them. A high count of appointment bookings, for example, shows the AI is actively converting callers into scheduled customers.

## Average Call Duration

For voice-enabled rooftops, the **Average Call Duration** KPI reflects the median length of AI-handled calls in the selected time window. A shorter average duration is not inherently better — the right length depends on your call types. Use this metric alongside the AI Actions breakdown to understand whether calls are ending because customers got what they needed or because they dropped off early.

<Tip>
  Use the **Last 7 Days** view to spot week-over-week trends and identify which days drive the most interaction volume. Switch to **Last 30 Days** for monthly reporting — it gives you a clean four-week picture that aligns well with typical performance review cycles.
</Tip>
